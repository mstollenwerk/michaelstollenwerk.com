Methodology

Each trading day runs 17:00 ET → 17:00 ET, with a fixed 16:55–18:15 ET window always excluded — the daily New York rollover stretch responsible for most single-tick bad prints — along with any bar marked erase in manual review. Every excluded or missing minute is filled by geometric/log-linear interpolation between the nearest real closes.

The bias-adjusted realized-covariance estimator (Zhang/Mykland/Aït-Sahalia, 2005) combines 5-minute and 1-minute sampling to cancel microstructure noise. A day is only included if every pair has at least 360 genuinely observed minutes after exclusions; 4 of 208 days in this range were dropped on that basis.
